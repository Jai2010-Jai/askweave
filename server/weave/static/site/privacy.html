Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: username, the name you want to be called, and the email address and account ID of the Google account you sign in with.
  • Phone: the WhatsApp number you connect, so Weave can reply to you.
  • Messages and projects: what you send Weave and the apps and sites it builds, so it can keep working on them.
  • Usage: number of builds and the amount of AI processing used, to enforce limits and control costs.

Who processes it

  • Twilio and WhatsApp (Meta) deliver messages.
  • Google Gemini generates apps and sites from your requests.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to our server.

We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

Public sites

Websites you build are published at your address on askweave.xyz and are visible to anyone with the link.

Visitors to your site

So we can tell you whether anyone is seeing your site, we count the visits to it ourselves. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising network and no other outside company involved, and your visitors get no cookies from us.

For each visit we record which page was opened and when, whether it was a phone or a computer, the country if Cloudflare tells us, and the website someone came from (just the site's name, like "instagram.com", never the full address). We do not store your visitors' IP addresses or the details of their browser.

To count people rather than page views we turn each visitor's address and browser into a scrambled code, using a secret that is kept only in our server's memory and thrown away and replaced every day. Once the day is over that code cannot be turned back into anyone, even by us. The secret is different for every site, so the same person visiting two Weave sites cannot be recognised as the same person.

This means we can tell you how many people came on a given day, but we cannot tell you whether someone who came last week has come back. Single visits are kept for about five weeks; after that only the daily totals remain, for a little over a year. You can switch the counting off for your sites at any time from your dashboard.

Messages sent through a site

If you fill in a contact form on a Weave-built site, your name, the email or phone you give and your message are passed to the owner of that site, and shown to them on their dashboard. We keep a copy only so they can read it later. We don't use it for anything else and we don't send you marketing.

The site owner, not Weave, decides what happens with your message after that.

Your choices

  • Turn visitor counting for your sites on or off from the dashboard.
  • Download all your data from the dashboard at any time.
  • Delete projects (restorable for a limited time) or delete your whole account from the dashboard.
  • Text STOP to Weave to stop receiving messages.

Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies. Hosted sites run sandboxed and can't send data anywhere.
